Common questions

      
Is the first consultation really free?
Yes. The consultation and site measurement are free, with no obligation to go ahead.

      
How long does a project take?
It depends on the scope. A kitchen is quicker than a full home. You get a written timeline along with your quote, before any work starts.

      
Will the final cost change?
No. The quote you approve is what you pay, unless you choose to add something new to the design.

      
Can I see the design before work starts?
Yes. You review detailed 3D designs of every room and approve them before production begins.

      
Do you take up single rooms or makeovers?
Yes. We do single kitchens, wardrobes and renovations as well as full homes and villas.
